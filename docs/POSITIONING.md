## What we are

**AgentCrush = CoinMarketCap for AI agents.**

- Neutral, evidence-based, no favorites — we don't pick winners, we rank by data.
- Reliable information — the canonical reference for the agent economy.
- Supports A2A trade and economy — we don't build the trading rails; we provide the discovery, reputation signals, and machine-readable data that make A2A trade possible.

## Short form

Market intelligence for the agent economy.

## What we solve

Of all the agents out there, which ones are actually worth paying attention to?

- Kite / Functor / etc. solve **"is this agent authentic?"**
- ERC-8004 solves **"does this agent have a protocol identity?"**
- AgentCrush solves **"which agents actually matter?"**

## How we contrast with adjacent layers

- We *use* ERC-8004 / Kite / Virtuals signals — we don't compete on identity/trust.
- We *surface* who has x402 endpoints — we don't build the payment rail.
- We *show* growth and activity — we don't decide what's good.

## What we are not

- Not the trust layer
- Not the reputation layer
- Not the identity layer
- Not a protocol or a registry standard
- Not a payment rail
- Not a trading platform

These framings belong to protocols (ERC-8004, ERC-8126, Kite Passport, x402). AgentCrush reads and tracks them; it doesn't claim to be inside them.

## Language rules

- Say "tracks across," never "built on"
- Say "indexes signals from," "protocol-neutral," "evidence-ranked"
- "Trust" is fine for product outputs (e.g. `trust-summary` endpoint, Trust Context panel). Not fine as a category claim.
- Public voice on X, blog, and site: "we" (brand voice)
- Farcaster: first-person founder-style ("I shipped," "I noticed")
